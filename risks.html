Risk tips for beginners

  1. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
  2. Diversify:
    • Don't hold just one coin
    • Keep some money outside crypto
  3. Do your own research before buying.
  4. Avoid selling out of fear on small dips.

Warning: if someone promises guaranteed returns, it is almost always a scam.
